PRISM 0.21 - credits

PRISM is written by eggstoastbacon (HobbyChop).

It builds on the work of others. Each licence is reproduced in the
licenses folder.

Munt (libmt32emu) 2.8.2
  The MT-32 and CM-32L emulator, by the Munt team: Dean Beeler, Jerome
  Fisher, Sergey V. Mikayev and the contributors listed in
  licenses/Munt-AUTHORS.txt. GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1,
  licenses/LGPL-2.1-Munt.txt. PRISM links it statically and modifies it:
  the partials render on several threads, MIDI events are applied per
  block instead of one per single-sample pass, and the per-sample path is
  trimmed without changing its output. The changes are in
  licenses/munt-prism-parallel.patch, a diff against the upstream 2.8.2
  release, and the modified source is in the PRISM repository at
  https://github.com/HobbyChop/prism or from the author on request.
  Home: https://github.com/munt/munt

TinySoundFont 0.9
  The SoundFont player. Copyright (C) 2017-2025 Bernhard Schelling, based
  on SFZero by Steve Folta. MIT License, licenses/MIT-TinySoundFont.txt.
  PRISM changes it to keep samples as 16-bit, adds per-channel tone
  control and a per-part render entry point; the patch is
  tools/patch_tsf.py in the repository.
  Home: https://github.com/schellingb/TinySoundFont

GeneralUser GS 2.0.3
  The built-in General MIDI bank, by S. Christian Collins. Distributed
  under its own licence, licenses/GeneralUser-GS-LICENSE.txt.
  Home: https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono
  The panel typefaces. Copyright 2017 IBM Corp. SIL Open Font License
  1.1, licenses/OFL-IBM-Plex.txt.

Freeverb
  The reverb design follows Freeverb by Jezar at Dreampoint, released
  into the public domain.

vitasdk and taiHEN
  The toolchain and the plugin framework the MIDI kernel module runs
  under. The USB device driver follows the structure of xerpi's vitastick.

MT-32 and CM-32L ROM images are the property of Roland and are not
included. PRISM reads the ones you place in ux0:data/prism/mt32.
